A night with us

How a session actually runs.

7:30 PM

Arrival & setup

Meet your guide, get a red-light headlamp, and let your eyes begin adjusting to the dark.

8:15 PM

Naked-eye orientation

Learn to read the sky before the optics come out — bright stars, visible planets, the ecliptic line.

9:00 PM

Telescope viewing

Rotate through three telescopes, each aimed at a different target for that night's conditions.

10:30 PM

Wrap-up

Warm drinks, a star chart to take home, and next month's dates for anyone who wants to come back.
